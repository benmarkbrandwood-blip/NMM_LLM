## Meaning of the decision

The three strata have enough independently audited source candidates, and no
evidence supports assigning one source a larger evidentiary weight. The Book
stratum receives the indivisible extra place. Every later report must show the
three stratum results separately before any combined summary.

This decision freezes only the counts. It does not yet freeze:

- the allocation of the 22 Book places between Sanmill named variations and
  the expert-curated subtype;
- any individual prefix;
- the final 64-prefix identity or review images; or
- an evaluation, training, publication, or promotion launch.

The separately frozen
[33-pattern Expert Book catalogue](sanmill-layered-expert-book-coverage-decision-2026-08-01.md)
remains intact. A balanced-core subset does not remove patterns from that
diagnostic catalogue.

## Membership derivation boundary

Membership remains source-only and candidate-independent:

1. Book selection covers every Sanmill declared family and every distinct
   expert eight-ply parent before allocating an additional child. It rejects
   exact-history, exact-FEN, and D4/`ring16` duplication under the documented
   deterministic tiebreaks.
2. HumanDB selection follows the already frozen ordering of distinct-game
   count, occurrence count, and history identity. It uses only complete
   histories that occurred in the current PlayOK sample and skips structures
   already selected for Book.
3. Perfect DB selection uses the existing 128-route StrictSteps audit pool. It
   does not regenerate routes after seeing a model or another result.

The placement-prefix layer still does not replace the movement/flying corpus.
No candidate model is loaded and no game is played by recording this decision.
